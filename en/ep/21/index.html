Page 16

A business that takes money to put people up needs a permit under the Hotel and Inn Business Act!!

Note: Running a lodging business for a fee requires a permit from the prefectural governor (or the mayor of a city with a public health center) under the Hotel and Inn Business Act.

Futon fee, electricity fee, it's all a lodging fee.

Even minpaku needs a notification, and a cap of 180 days a year.

Note: Minpaku (home lodging) is run under the Private Lodging Business Act: file a notification, and operate up to 180 days a year. A tenant must also attach the landlord's written consent to subletting.

And without the landlord's consent, the residents get evicted!

A futon fee is a futon fee!!
