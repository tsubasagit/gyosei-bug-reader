Page 1

October. Nashigasaka City Hall, the basement.

The main building's Industry Division wants an inspection visit...

A factory in Niihama City, Ehime.

A business trip!! I'm going!!

Inspection = looking = vacation!!

An inspection report... my name gets known at the main building!!

I've got school, so I'll hold the fort. Paid hourly.

I'm taking paid leave that week. More of my pilgrimage.

Page 2

Across the Seto Ohashi Bridge: Niihama, Ehime.

...Swim ring?

Inspection, right? I'll inspect the sea.

Niihama is a factory town that grew up with the Besshi Copper Mine.

Mining began in 1691 and ended in 1973. 283 years.

So basically, it's a town with good food!!

LISTEN TO ME!!

...The same numbers, on two sheets?

Measure it, write it on the check sheet, then in the daily log too.

The same numbers TWICE!?

Page 5

Every part number gets measured in different spots.

Four hundred kinds of check sheets alone.

And every night, I type all those sheets back in.

The same numbers... three times.

THREE TIMES!?

Page 6

If you brought in a system, the retyping...

Millions of yen, months of work. Won't be ready by tomorrow.

We're closed for the three festival days, so it ships tomorrow.

This year my grandson carries the float for the first time.

Note: The Niihama Taiko Festival (Oct. 16-18) is famous for its taikodai: giant drum floats of about three tons, carried on the shoulders of some 150 bearers.

Tonight's practice... with this pile, no way.

LUNCH!!
