    The Basement Strikes Back

    Inter-Department Sports Day (Office Olympics)

    It's autumn, and City Hall's inter-department sports day is on. The winning section gets to pick any empty room in the building. Tetsu wants a corner room for napping; Rena wants to beat her elite rival Himuro. But the basement section has only four members. Then Tetsu decides the delivery guy who stops by every day is "practically one of us"...

    AI? Already Installed

    AI for Small Businesses (Auto-Pilot)

    "We haven't even installed AI, and it already scares us." The shopkeepers of the local shopping street come down to the basement office. Tetsu, woken from his nap, snaps at them, half-sleeps through Rena's security lecture, then names himself an "AI consultant" and, for 30,000 yen a month, hands every shop's phone over to AI.

    The Nobel Prize

    The son of an old Chinese diner wants to sell trendy mala tang by weight, and after being bounced between counters he ends up in the basement section. Selling by weight requires a government-certified scale. "Certified? I'll certify it myself!!" Tetsu decides a City Hall stamp can leap right over national law...

Seeing the craftsmen write every measurement twice on paper, Tetsu spots the slogan "No Double Work" and has an idea...

"I'll make the circulars for all 120 associations!" Tetsu sets out to mass-produce 120 copies in one night with "Replace All."

    Set one floor below City Hall

    Welcome to the Special Trends Unit

    In the basement of Nashigasaka City Hall sits a unit with no budget and no authority. Every time a citizen’s problem lands there, Tetsu Genda — a civil servant who thinks about nothing but money and naps — smells profit and starts moving.

    His methods are mostly outrageous. And yet the problems keep getting solved.

    Four difficult people

    The team downstairs

    • Lead · staff

      Tetsu Genda38

      “This... this could be money!”

      Driven entirely by cash and the urge to slack off. When he smells a profit he can learn any rule or any technology overnight. Helping people is a side effect.

    • Team leader · Tetsu’s boss

      Rena Akamine

      “Tetsu. Is that actually legal?”

      Logical, sharp, and the only person who tries to stop Tetsu. She is usually right, and usually one step too late.

    • High-school part-timer

      Shota Suzuki16

      “If it pays, I’ll do it. No overtime.”

      Does the math before he does anything. He joins Tetsu’s schemes strictly by the numbers — and his refusal to work late is backed by actual labor law.

    • Section chief

      Go Gotokuji46

      “Genda. ...I mean, Mr. Genda.”

      Never raises his voice. The angrier he gets, the more polite he becomes, and the brighter his glasses shine. If he calls you “Mr.”, run.
